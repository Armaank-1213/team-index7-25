# 🧠 Clariti

## What It Is
Clariti is an **app** designed to help detect early signs of cognitive decline by tracking daily habits and health information, alongside regular cognitive testing. Our mission is to help people facing cognitive decline get diagnosed earlier so they can be treated more effectively.

## The Problem We Solve
Many people facing cognitive decline struggle to get diagnosed early enough for treatment to be effective. Clariti aims to close that gap by making it easy to track relevant daily habits and flag concerning patterns before they become bigger problems.

## Who It's For
- Elderly individuals
- People at risk for dementia or other cognitive/mental illnesses

## Platform
**App** (built with Python and Streamlit)

## Features We Want to Build
- 📝 Daily logging of health habits (sleep, exercise, water intake, etc.)
- 🧩 Brain games
- 🧠 Cognitive testing
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctor referrals

## Sprint 1 Feature (Completed 7/25)
**Cognitive Wellness Dashboard** — a daily check-in dashboard where users log steps, exercise, sleep, and brain games, see a wellness score, and get simple feedback on their progress.
